## UX contract

Every operational screen must answer five questions without opening another page:

1. What is this item?
2. Which canonical state is it in?
3. Why is it in this state?
4. Who or what must act next?
5. What is the economic or operational risk of proceeding?

## Interaction principles

- Expensive processing never starts implicitly.
- Approval actions show their consequence before confirmation.
- Empty, loading, stale, partial, failed, and blocked are distinct states.
- Business audit context is visible near critical actions.
- Supplier and channel details are adapter data, not the organizing model of the interface.
- Dense information is presented through hierarchy, not decorative cards.
- Tables are used for exact comparison; boards are used for flow and work queues.
